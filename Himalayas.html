Himalayas Trek in Nepal - Must See Routes

Everest Base Camp

Trek to the base of the world’s highest peak, Mount Everest. This legendary route offers breathtaking views of the Khumbu Glacier, Sherpa villages, and iconic peaks like Ama Dablam and Lhotse.

Annapurna Circuit

This diverse trail circles the Annapurna Massif, crossing Thorong La Pass (5,416m). It’s known for dramatic landscapes, cultural villages, and breathtaking views of peaks like Annapurna I and Dhaulagiri.

Langtang Valley

Often called the "valley of glaciers," Langtang offers a shorter but scenic trek with views of Langtang Lirung. You’ll explore Tamang villages and Buddhist monasteries close to Kathmandu.

Manaslu Circuit

A remote and challenging trek around the world’s 8th highest peak, Mt. Manaslu. It’s known for its off-the-beaten-path feel, suspension bridges, and Larkya La Pass.

Gosaikunda Trek

This spiritual trek takes you to the sacred alpine lake of Gosaikunda, revered by both Hindus and Buddhists. Ideal for a short Himalayan adventure near the Langtang range.

Upper Mustang

Explore the hidden Buddhist kingdom of Lo, with arid landscapes and ancient caves. Upper Mustang offers a culturally rich trek through medieval villages, monasteries, and desert-like beauty.
