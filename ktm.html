Visit Kathmandu - Top Places to Explore

Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple)

Swayambhunath is one of the oldest and most iconic religious sites in Kathmandu. Perched atop a hill, it offers panoramic views of the city. The complex includes a stupa, various shrines, and a monastery. The site is famous for its resident monkeys and the beautiful prayer wheels surrounding the stupa. Visitors often climb the 365 steps to reach the summit and experience a serene spiritual atmosphere.

Pashupatinath Temple

Pashupatinath is one of the holiest Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Shiva, located on the banks of the Bagmati River. It attracts thousands of pilgrims, especially during Maha Shivaratri festival. The temple complex is vast with many smaller temples, statues, and ashrams. Photography inside the main temple is restricted, but visitors can explore the surrounding ghats where cremations are held as part of Hindu tradition.

Boudhanath Stupa

Boudhanath Stupa is one of the largest spherical stupas in Nepal and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is a major pilgrimage site for Buddhists worldwide. The white dome with the painted eyes of Buddha is an iconic sight. The stupa is surrounded by monasteries and shops selling Tibetan handicrafts. Visitors can walk clockwise around the stupa while spinning prayer wheels for blessings.

Kathmandu Durbar Square

Kathmandu Durbar Square is a historic palace complex in the heart of the old city. It showcases traditional Newari architecture, with ornate temples, courtyards, and royal palaces. The area was severely affected by the 2015 earthquake but remains a cultural hotspot with ongoing restoration. Visitors can explore museums, shops, and cafes around the square, immersing themselves in Kathmandu's rich heritage.

Chandragiri Cable Car Top Station

As you embark on your journey to the hilltop, the Chandragiri Hills cable car gently carries you above the verdant pine forests, opening awe-inspiring panoramic views of the valley below, the lush hills surrounding it, and the majestic Himalayan range. The picturesque ride sets the tone for a truly unforgettable experience that awaits you..

Garden of Dreams

The Garden of Dreams, a neo classical historical garden, is situated in the midst of Kathmandu city. The Garden was famous as the garden of Six Seasons which was created by late Field Marshal Kaiser Sumsher Rana (1892-1964) in early 1920. After the completion of this Garden, it was considered as one of the most sophisticated private gardens of that time.
