Top Hotels in Pokhara

Hotel Barahi

Hotel Barahi offers stunning views of Phewa Lake and the Annapurna mountain range. Enjoy comfortable rooms with modern amenities, a rooftop restaurant, and easy access to Lakeside's attractions.

Temple Tree Resort & Spa

A tranquil boutique hotel nestled in a lush garden setting. Temple Tree Resort & Spa offers luxurious rooms, an award-winning spa, and delicious dining options just minutes from the city center.

Fishtail Lodge

Located on the edge of Phewa Lake, Fishtail Lodge blends traditional Nepalese architecture with modern comforts. Enjoy lakeside dining, boating facilities, and spectacular sunset views.

Waterfront Resort

Set on the lakeside promenade, Waterfront Resort features spacious rooms, a swimming pool, and direct lake access. Perfect for travelers seeking relaxation with breathtaking views.

Hotel Middle Path & Spa

A charming hotel located close to the city center, featuring elegant rooms, a wellness spa, and personalized services designed to make your stay memorable.

Hyatt Regency Kathmandu

A luxurious 5-star hotel near Boudhanath Stupa, Hyatt Regency offers lush gardens, a full-service spa, and an outdoor pool. Known for its elegant architecture and world-class hospitality, it’s perfect for travelers looking for comfort and serenity in the capital.

Hotel Mulberry

Located in Thamel, Hotel Mulberry features a rooftop infinity pool, spa services, and modern interiors. It’s ideal for both business travelers and vacationers seeking luxury in the bustling heart of Kathmandu.

Hotel Yak & Yeti

Set in a restored palace from the 19th century, Hotel Yak & Yeti combines heritage architecture with modern amenities. It boasts multiple restaurants, tennis courts, and an outdoor pool, making it a popular choice among dignitaries and tourists alike.

Hotel Shambala

A boutique hotel offering Tibetan-inspired décor and a rooftop pool with mountain views. Hotel Shambala is known for its personalized service, modern comfort, and peaceful ambiance—perfect for a relaxed Kathmandu stay.

Everest View Hotel, Namche Bazaar

Nestled at 3,880 meters, this hotel offers luxurious accommodations with views of Mount Everest. Enjoy cozy rooms, local cuisine, and a warm Sherpa welcome. Ideal for acclimatization and comfort during Everest Base Camp treks.

Ghorepani Guest House

Located in the heart of the Annapurna range, this family-run guesthouse offers breathtaking sunrise views from nearby Poon Hill. Warm hospitality, delicious meals, and essential trek facilities included.
